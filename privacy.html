P.H. WORKS INC. ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information obtained through our website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that visitors voluntarily provide through our website's forms.

Service Requests

When requesting plumbing services, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, property address, building type, service description, photos you choose to upload, and other information relevant to your request.

Quote Requests

When requesting an estimate or quotation, you may provide your name, company, contact information, project location, project details, a link to your plans, and other information necessary to evaluate your request.

Employment Applications

When applying for employment, you may provide your name, contact information, employment history, qualifications, certifications, and résumé.

Uploaded résumés may contain additional personal information voluntarily provided by applicants.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, banking information, government identification numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information through our website forms.

Technical Information

Our website hosting provider and the services that process form submissions may automatically process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and access logs, for operational and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to service requests and customer inquiries.

  • Review project information and prepare quotations.

  • Schedule and coordinate plumbing services.

  • Communicate with customers about requested services.

  • Review employment applications and contact applicants.

  • Maintain appropriate business records.

  • Operate and secure our website.

  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting a website form does not automatically establish a contractual relationship with P.H. WORKS INC.

3. Form Processing, Storage, and Email Delivery

When you submit a form on our website, the information is sent to our systems and stored in a database so our team can review and respond to it. We use the following service providers for this:

  • Supabase provides the database that stores form submissions and the private file storage used for uploaded photos and résumés.

  • Resend sends email on our behalf, including notifications to our team and automatic reply emails to people who submit a request.

Uploaded photos and résumés are kept in private storage and are not publicly accessible.

Stored submissions and uploaded files can be viewed only by authorized P.H. WORKS INC. staff with approved logins, and access is limited by role. For example, employment applications are available to hiring personnel, and service and estimate requests are available to the staff who handle them.

Email notifications and attachments delivered to our company email accounts may be retained for legitimate business purposes.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our hosting, database, and email providers may also process or store information as necessary to operate their services.

For more information, please review the privacy policies of Supabase and Resend.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected through our website.

We may disclose information to authorized employees, representatives, contractors, or service providers when reasonably necessary to respond to requests, evaluate applications, or deliver services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or governmental authority.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not currently use Google Analytics or advertising tracking pixels.

We use basic spam-protection measures on our forms, such as hidden fields that help detect automated submissions.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers and your IP address may be processed by Google when a page loads.

Our website hosting provider and other essential service providers may also use cookies or similar technologies for functionality and security.

If additional analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are introduced, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

6. Employment Applications

Information provided through our Careers page, including uploaded résumés, is used for recruitment and employment-related purposes.

Application materials may be accessed by authorized company personnel responsible for hiring.

We may retain application information for recruitment, business recordkeeping, and compliance with applicable employment laws.

Submitting an application does not guarantee employment.

7. Data Security

We seek to maintain reasonable measures appropriate to our operations to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and misuse.

However, no website, electronic communication, or storage system is completely secure.

Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through website forms.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Retention periods may depend on the nature of the information, business requirements, and applicable legal obligations.

Information may be deleted or securely disposed of when no longer reasonably necessary, subject to applicable retention requirements.

9. Privacy Requests

Individuals may contact P.H. WORKS INC. with questions or requests concerning information submitted through our website.

Requests for access, correction, or deletion will be reviewed and addressed in accordance with applicable laws and legitimate business requirements.

Certain information may need to be retained for legal compliance or other permitted purposes.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites.

We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of independently operated websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before submitting information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for individuals and businesses seeking plumbing services, quotations, or employment opportunities.

It is not directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, business practices, technology, or applicable laws.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective or last-updated date.
